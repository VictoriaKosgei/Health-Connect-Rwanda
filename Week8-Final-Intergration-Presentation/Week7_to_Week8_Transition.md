**Track:** Data Analytics  
**Project:** HealthConnect Clinic Experience Lab  
**Project Title:** Improving Patient Appointment Attendance and Healthcare Support Using Data and AI  
**Intern:** Victoria Kosgei

## 1. Main Week 7 Output

Completed KPI validation, data quality checks, analytical findings validation, and interactive dashboard functionality testing for the six-page HealthConnect Rwanda Power BI executive dashboard.

## 2. Most Important Testing Result

The principal financial KPIs were checked against their source tables. Dashboard interactions, including date, department and gender slicers, KPI responsiveness, chart cross-filtering, navigation and filter reset, passed testing.

## 3. Major Issue Discovered

The billing-to-appointment relationship had a significant matching limitation: 55,000 of 60,000 billing records were unmatched to appointment records.

The appointment dataset also does not contain an attendance status field, preventing reliable attendance and no-show rate calculations.

## 4. What Was Improved

The Week 7 work clarified the distinction between gross billed revenue and department-allocated revenue, validated the three appointment visit types, corrected the patient gender distribution, and documented data limitations and recommendations.

## 5. Component Considered Ready

The six-page Power BI analytics dashboard and its validated analytical outputs are ready for final presentation, subject to the documented limitations.

## 6. What Still Needs Integration

The outstanding areas include billing-to-appointment matching, facility mapping, attendance data availability, and the connection between validated analytics findings and the wider predictive and administrative support components.

## 7. Tracks Involved

The Data Analytics contribution needs to connect with Data Science, ML Engineering, Generative AI and Project Management through the HC-POD final integration process.

## 8. Intended Final Presentation

Demonstrate the healthcare analytics dashboard, explain the validated KPIs and appointment visit-type distribution, present important financial and data quality findings, explain cross-track contributions, and communicate the business recommendations and limitations.
