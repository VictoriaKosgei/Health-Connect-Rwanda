## 1. Final Component

My final component is the **HealthConnect Rwanda Healthcare Analytics and Business Intelligence Dashboard**, developed using Microsoft Power BI.

The component includes a six-page interactive executive dashboard covering:

- Executive Overview
- Clinical Outcomes
- Patient Demographics
- Financial Performance
- Operational Performance
- Patient Experience

It provides validated analytical outputs, KPI measures, business insights, and recommendations based on the available healthcare datasets.

## 2. Business Problem Addressed

HealthConnect Clinic seeks to reduce missed appointments, improve patient appointment attendance, make better use of available appointment slots, and provide effective administrative support.

The analytics component supports these objectives by providing visibility into appointment patterns, patient demographics, clinical records, financial performance, and data quality.

The current dataset does not contain attendance status, so the dashboard cannot directly measure attendance or no-show rates.

## 9. Remaining Limitations

The following limitations remain documented:

1. 55,000 billing records are unmatched to appointment records.
2. Approximately 5% facility mapping limits facility-level analysis.
3. The appointment dataset does not contain an attendance status field.
4. Bed occupancy history is unavailable.
5. Reliable room occupancy data is unavailable.
6. Equipment usage and downtime history are insufficient for utilization analysis.
7. Financial reconciliation and Revenue Summary differences require further investigation.
8. The Data Science emergency prediction model did not meet its reported release criteria.

These limitations must be communicated during the final presentation and must not be presented as resolved.
