# HealthConnect Rwanda – Week 7: Analytics Testing & Refinement

## Project Overview
Week 7 of the AnalystLab Africa Internship focused on testing, KPI validation, analytical accuracy and dashboard refinement for the HealthConnect Rwanda Data Analytics Project.

Building on Weeks 5 and 6, the objective was to verify existing analytical outputs, investigate inconsistencies and improve dashboard reliability and business interpretation.

## Key Activities
- Reviewed Week 6 analytical outputs.
- Validated important KPI calculations.
- Compared dashboard values against underlying datasets.
- Reviewed dashboard filters, calculations and interactions.
- Reassessed appointment attendance and no-show findings.
- Investigated analytical inconsistencies.
- Omitted blank values from relevant analysis during data preparation rather than treating them as valid values.
- Reviewed findings across available segments.
- Refined dashboard visualisations and business interpretation.
- Updated recommendations and documented analytical limitations.
- Prepared findings for cross-track coordination and Week 8 integration.

## Testing and Validation
Testing focused on:
1. KPI accuracy.
2. Dashboard consistency.
3. Data completeness and blank-value handling.
4. Appointment and no-show analysis.
5. Diagnosis and financial analysis.
6. Dashboard filters and interactions.
7. Visualisation clarity.
8. Business insight reliability.

## Key Data Considerations
- 5,000 appointment records.
- 20,000 patient profiles.
- Approximately 60,000 diagnosis records.
- Blank values were omitted from relevant analysis during data preparation.
- Appointment and financial datasets have differences in record coverage and linkage.
- Some facility-level and insurance analyses have documented limitations.

## Deliverables
- Testing and validation record.
- KPI validation documentation.
- Dashboard testing evidence.
- Refined visualisations.
- Updated business recommendations.
- Analytical limitations.
- Week 8 recommendations.

## Conclusion
Week 7 strengthened the analytical quality assurance process by focusing on the accuracy, consistency and usefulness of existing HealthConnect Rwanda analytical outputs.
