# HealthConnect Rwanda – Analytical Findings Validation

## 1. Purpose

This document records key analytical findings reviewed during Week 7 to confirm that the dashboard's business interpretations were consistent with the available data.

## 2. Patient Demographics

The validated gender distribution was:

| Gender | Percentage |
|---|---:|
| Male | 50.45% |
| Female | 49.55% |

The figures show a nearly balanced gender distribution across the analyzed patient records.

## 3. Diagnosis Analysis

The diagnosis records were reviewed and the following counts were identified:

| Diagnosis | Records |
|---|---:|
| Gastritis | 12,208 |
| Diabetes | 12,083 |
| Hypertension | 12,075 |
| Malaria | 11,878 |
| Upper Respiratory Infection | 11,756 |

These values represent diagnosis records and should not automatically be interpreted as unique patients or disease prevalence.

## 4. Chronic Condition Analysis

| Chronic Condition | Records |
|---|---:|
| Asthma | 4,996 |
| Hypertension | 4,988 |
| Diabetes | 4,945 |

These counts describe records in the available chronic-condition data.

## 5. Insurance Analysis

The insurance claim approval rate was counter-checked at 49.98%.

The interpretation of claim statuses must account for the available categories, including Pending and blank values. Blank statuses should not automatically be treated as rejected claims.

## 6. Financial Findings

The financial analysis identified:

- Gross billed revenue of RWF 9.31 billion.
- Payments collected of RWF 8.39 billion.
- Department allocated revenue of approximately RWF 771.13 million.

The difference in analytical scope is important because 55,000 billing records were unmatched to appointment records.

## 7. Resource Utilization Limitations

The available data did not provide sufficient evidence to calculate actual bed, room or equipment utilization.

The bed records showed 2,000 beds marked as Available. However, availability alone does not establish actual occupancy or utilization.

Similarly, equipment records did not provide sufficient usage or downtime history to calculate a reliable utilization rate.

## 8. Outstanding Analytical Validation

Attendance and no-show analysis remains to be confirmed using the actual appointment status values.

Appointment types such as Follow-up, Consultation and Emergency describe the type of appointment, not whether the patient attended.

## 9. Conclusion

The reviewed findings support the current dashboard interpretation within the limits of the available data.

Further validation is required for attendance analysis and resource utilization before those areas can be reported as complete.
